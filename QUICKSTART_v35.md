# 🎯 AIR TOUCH INTERFACE v3.5 - QUICK START GUIDE

## 📦 Installation & Setup

### Option 1: Quick Start (Recommended for Most Users)

1. **Double-click** `AirTouch.bat`
   - This launches the professional settings menu
   - No technical knowledge required!

### Option 2: Direct Launch

- **Double-click** `start_airtouch.bat` for immediate application start

---

## 🚀 First Run

1. Open **AirTouch.bat** or **start_airtouch.bat**
2. You'll see the professional launcher menu
3. Select **"1️⃣ START AIR TOUCH INTERFACE"**
4. Position your hand in front of camera
5. You should see your hand being detected!

---

## 🎮 BASIC USAGE

### Moving the Cursor
- **Raise your right hand** in front of camera
- **Move your hand** to move cursor
- Cursor follows smoothly
- Works best at arm's length distance

### Clicking
- **Position cursor** over target
- **Push hand forward** (toward camera) quickly
- You'll hear a click sound
- For right-click: push with **two fingers** or use menu

### Scrolling
- **Raise both hands** in front of camera
- **Move hands up/down** to scroll
- Works in any scrollable window

## ⚙️ CONFIGURATION

### Access Settings
In launcher menu, select: **"2️⃣ SETTINGS & CONFIGURATION"**

You can adjust:
- ✅ Cursor speed
- ✅ Smoothing and stability
- ✅ Click sensitivity
- ✅ Themes (Dark, Light, Neon, etc.)
- ✅ Camera settings
- ✅ Hand dominance (Left/Right/Auto)

### Quick Profiles
Instead of manual configuration, use predefined profiles:

| Profile | Best For | Characteristics |
|---------|----------|-----------------|
| **Gaming** | Fast-paced interaction | High speed, responsive |
| **Precision** | Careful clicking | Slow, stable, smooth |
| **Navigation** | Web browsing | Balanced, comfortable |
| **Accessibility** | Mobility assistance | Extra stable, large cursor |

## 🆘 TROUBLESHOOTING

### Hand Not Detected?
1. Check camera (Menu → Diagnostics)
2. Ensure good lighting
3. Move hand closer to camera
4. Try **Low Light Mode** in Settings

### Cursor Jumps Around?
1. Increase smoothing
2. Use **Precision Profile**
3. Check for reflections on camera
4. Increase stability level

### Click Not Working?
1. Ensure steady push toward camera
2. Adjust click sensitivity
3. Try different force settings
4. Check camera resolution

### Slow Performance?
1. Lower camera resolution
2. Use Power Saving mode
3. Close background apps
4. Run benchmark to check
